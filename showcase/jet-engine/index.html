Turbofan Engine

Intake → compression → combustion → exhaust

A twin-spool turbofan built as a training and display model. The walkthrough follows the air from the fan to the nozzle, X-ray shows the blade stages inside the casings, and the throttle drives fan speed and estimated thrust.

Open live 3D ↗ See it on your product

How it's built

What the demo shows

  • The working cycle, step by step Intake, compression, combustion and exhaust, each step framed and captioned while the rotors turn.
  • X-ray and scan views See the blade stages through the casings, or switch to the scan look for presentations.
  • Live throttle Move the throttle and watch fan speed N1 and estimated thrust follow.
  • Axial exploded view Separate the casings along the engine axis to show how the core is stacked.
