Worm Gear Reducer

90° turn · high reduction ratio · self-locking

A single-stage worm gear reducer, opened up. Buyers watch the worm drive the bronze wheel through the cut-away housing, follow a walkthrough of how the reduction works, and change the motor speed to see output speed and torque respond.

Open live 3D ↗ See it on your product

How it's built

What the demo shows

  • Cut-away housing The casing is open on one side, so the worm and the wheel are visible in mesh without switching views.
  • How the reduction works A captioned walkthrough turns the worm and the wheel together and explains the high ratio and why the drive self-locks.
  • Live input speed and power Set the motor speed and power; output speed and torque update while the model runs.
  • Exploded view and colours Pull the gearbox apart along its shafts, and switch the flange paint between RAL colours.
