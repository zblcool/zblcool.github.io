Ashmartisan Worm Gear Reducer▾ Get a quote

01

Worm (input shaft)

The screw driven directly by the motor. Its thread works like a screw: one full turn advances the worm wheel by just one tooth.

Starts
Single start
Material
Hardened alloy steel
Ask about this part →

02

Worm wheel

The helical gear that meshes with the worm. Its tooth count sets the ratio: more teeth mean a slower output and more torque.

Material
Tin bronze
Ratio
≈ teeth ÷ worm starts
Ask about this part →

03

Output shaft

Fixed to the worm wheel, it delivers the multiplied torque to the load. It runs at right angles to the input worm.

Orientation
90° to the input shaft
Ask about this part →

04

Motor flange

Standard mounting interface: the motor bolts on here and its shaft goes into the worm to drive the unit.

Mounting
6 bolts
Ask about this part →

05

Housing

The cast casing supports both shafts and keeps the mesh accurate. It is filled with lubricating oil and also dissipates the heat.

Material
Cast iron / aluminium alloy

What buyers can do

Click a part, learn what it does

Numbered parts open a short explanation and the specifications that matter, with the camera moving to the part.

See inside

X-ray, section and blueprint views show the internals without a cut-away drawing.

Take it apart

Exploded views and step-by-step assembly show how the parts fit and in which order.

Watch it work

Guided walkthroughs animate the working principle, one step at a time, with captions.

Try the numbers

Operating parameters respond live, so buyers see what speed, pressure or ratio change.

Ask with context

"Get a quote" sends your sales team what the buyer looked at, so the first reply can be specific.
