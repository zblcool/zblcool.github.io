Demonstration project. Built by Ashmartisan to show the process. Not a client project: no customer, no customer CAD and no commercial results. The pump is generic, drawn from ISO 2858 and IEC 60072 dimensions; its figures and options are illustrative.

Stage 01 / 04

Original CAD

We start from whatever engineering data you have: a STEP or native CAD export, a BIM object, drawings, or just photos of the product. No 3D model yet? We build it for you. Then we agree with your engineers which parts buyers need to understand.

In this demonstration

There is no customer CAD in this project. We modelled the pump from the ISO 2858 and IEC 60072 dimension tables, the same way we would work from your drawings or photos.

Optimised 3D

Stage 03 / 04

Interactive experience

We turn the model into something buyers use: numbered parts with plain-language explanations, X-ray, section and exploded views, a guided walkthrough, and a configurator that only allows valid options.
