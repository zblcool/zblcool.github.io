Your existing website

Embed an interactive 3D product viewer in the pages you already have

You don't need a new website to show your products in 3D. The explainer goes into an existing product page as one block, next to your photos, price and buy button, with a single line of code.

Open the live demo ↗ Book a demo

Why it fits into an existing page

One line of code

An iframe snippet works in Shopify, WordPress, Webflow and any CMS that accepts embedded content.

Your page stays fast

Nothing heavy loads until the visitor clicks "View in 3D", and page scrolling passes over the block until then.

Fits the block it's in

The layout adapts to the space it is given, from a wide product gallery to a phone screen, with a full-screen button.

The full explainer inside

Parts, X-ray and exploded views, walkthroughs and a quote request, all inside the block.

Your brand

Your colours and your contact details: the embed carries no studio branding of its own.

Know where enquiries come from

Quote requests sent from the embed are marked as such, so you know which pages bring buyers.

FAQ

What people usually ask.

Will it slow down our product pages?

No. The page only loads a light placeholder; the 3D model loads when the visitor clicks it.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Give the block a taller shape on phone layouts and it switches to its phone layout, with full screen available.

Which website builders are supported?

Any that lets you add an iframe or custom HTML: Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix and most CMSs.

Can several products share one embed?

Each product has its own link. One project can cover your whole range, each product embedded on its own page.
