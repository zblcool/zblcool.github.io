Website · sales tablet

Interactive 3D product explainers for industrial equipment

Buyers don't read spec sheets the way engineers write them. An interactive 3D explainer lets them click a part to see what it does, look inside the housing, take the product apart and watch it run, on your product page or on a sales rep's tablet.

Open the live demo ↗ Book a demo

What your buyers can do

Click a part, learn what it does

Numbered parts open a short explanation and the specifications that matter, with the camera moving to the part.

See inside

X-ray, section and blueprint views show the internals without a cut-away drawing.

Take it apart

Exploded views and step-by-step assembly show how the parts fit and in which order.

Watch it work

Guided walkthroughs animate the working principle, one step at a time, with captions.

Try the numbers

Operating parameters respond live, so buyers see what speed, pressure or ratio change.

Ask with context

"Get a quote" sends your sales team what the buyer looked at, so the first reply can be specific.

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Your model

    We start from your CAD export or product model and prepare it for the browser: light enough for phones, detailed where it matters.

  2. 2

    Your story

    Together we decide which parts to explain, which walkthroughs to animate and which numbers buyers should be able to change.

  3. 3

    Your channels

    The same build goes on your website, on sales tablets and, if you want, on your trade-show screen.

A good fit for

  • Gearboxes, pumps, valves and compressors
  • Brakes, drivetrain and vehicle parts
  • Machines with moving internals
  • Products sold through distributors and dealers
  • Technical sales with long lead times

FAQ

What people usually ask.

What do buyers need to install?

Nothing. The explainer runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Can we use our CAD files?

Yes. An export from your CAD system (STEP, or a mesh format such as OBJ, FBX or glTF) is the usual starting point. We simplify it for the web and keep the parts you want to explain.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. It is multilingual, Spanish and more: adding a language means adding text, not code.

Who receives the enquiries?

Your sales team, by email or through the form service you already use, together with a summary of what the buyer looked at.
